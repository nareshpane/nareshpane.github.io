# Bibliography

The webpage gives direct links beside modern results. This file records the
main classic, computational, and reference sources used in the exposition.

1. Euler, L. (1744). *Variae observationes circa series infinitas*. Euler Archive E72. <https://scholarlycommons.pacific.edu/euler-works/72/>
2. Riemann, B. (1859). *Ueber die Anzahl der Primzahlen unter einer gegebenen Grösse*. Original and translation: <https://www.maths.tcd.ie/pub/HistMath/People/Riemann/Zeta/>
3. von Mangoldt, H. (1895). *Zu Riemanns Abhandlung Ueber die Anzahl der Primzahlen unter einer gegebenen Grösse*. <https://doi.org/10.1515/crll.1895.114.255>
4. Hadamard, J. (1896). *Sur la distribution des zéros de la fonction zêta(s) et ses conséquences arithmétiques*. <https://www.numdam.org/articles/10.24033/bsmf.545/>
5. von Mangoldt, H. (1905). *Zur Verteilung der Nullstellen der Riemannschen Funktion xi(t)*. <https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01447494>
6. Hardy, G. H. (1914). *Sur les zéros de la fonction zêta de Riemann*. <https://archive.org/details/comptesrendusheb158acad/page/1012/mode/2up>
7. Selberg, A. (1942). *On the zeros of Riemann's zeta-function*. Skrifter Norske Videnskaps-Akademi i Oslo I, no. 10.
8. Levinson, N. (1974). *More than one third of zeros of Riemann's zeta-function are on sigma=1/2*. <https://doi.org/10.1016/0001-8708(74)90074-7>
9. Montgomery, H. L. (1973). *The pair correlation of zeros of the zeta function*. <https://doi.org/10.1090/pspum/024/9944>
10. Apéry, R. (1979). *Irrationalité de zeta(2) et zeta(3)*. <https://www.numdam.org/item/AST_1979__61__11_0/>
11. Odlyzko, A. M. (1987). *On the distribution of spacings between zeros of the zeta function*. <https://doi.org/10.1090/S0025-5718-1987-0866115-0>
12. Conrey, J. B. (1989). *More than two fifths of the zeros of the Riemann zeta function are on the critical line*. <https://doi.org/10.1515/crll.1989.399.1>
13. Schoenfeld, L. (1976). *Sharper bounds for the Chebyshev functions theta(x) and psi(x). II*. <https://doi.org/10.1090/S0025-5718-1976-0457374-X>
14. NIST Digital Library of Mathematical Functions, Chapter 25. <https://dlmf.nist.gov/25>
15. Edwards, H. M. (1974). *Riemann's Zeta Function*. Academic Press.
16. Titchmarsh, E. C., revised by Heath-Brown, D. R. (1986). *The Theory of the Riemann Zeta-Function*, 2nd ed. Oxford University Press.
17. Iwaniec, H. and Kowalski, E. (2004). *Analytic Number Theory*. American Mathematical Society.

For sources checked specifically for the recent-results section, see
