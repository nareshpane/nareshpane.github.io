      Sources, methodology & limitations

      Observed merchandise, annual scope

      Source: the supplied Statistics Canada Canadian International Merchandise Trade (CIMT) domestic-export extract, ODPFN018_202512N.csv. Its header identifies HS6, destination, province, U.S. state, value, quantity and unit of measure. Alberta (AB) is the province-of-origin filter; all twelve months, January–December 2025, are summed. State-level U.S. observations are retained before aggregation. Monthly observations are distinct, not duplicates.

      The complete observation key is month × HS6 × country × province × state × unit of measure. The builder stops on repeated keys for review. It preserves six-character HS6 strings and leading zeros; HS4 is the first four characters. No monthly, quantity or predictive view is exposed.

      Destination rule

      Keep countries and separately identified territories whose lookup records are active in 2025, including Kosovo (XK). Exclude unknown/unspecified (ZX), High Seas (ZZ), and the ambiguous legacy grouped label Pacific Islands (PC). There are no World or continent totals in the active lookup. All three excluded codes have zero Alberta value, so the identified-market sum is the entire Alberta annual total. Antarctica is a separately reported geographic destination, not a sovereign country; it remains included. All positive flows remain in totals, search and product detail.

      Descriptions and classification

      HS4 labels use explicit heading rows, or inferred heading paths confirmed across all children. Exact appended tariff-rate notes are removed from labels and recorded in the audit. Formal HS4 heading labels come from Page 1's CBSA T2026-2 hierarchy, joined by exact string code. All active international HS4/HS6 codes match that hierarchy. Five national/special export headings (9802, 9806, 9807, 9812, 9901) do not match and use code-only labels. These national provisions remain in merchandise totals and are not ordinary international HS headings. HS6 labels use the Statistics Canada export description lookup active in 2025; none is missing. The later CBSA snapshot supplies classification labels, not 2025 tariff treatment; code matching does not independently certify unchanged wording between editions.

      Shares and concentration

      Country share = destination exports ÷ all Alberta exports × 100
      Top-four concentration = four largest HS4 values within a destination ÷ destination total × 100

      Destinations and products are ranked by annual CAD value, descending, with string-code order breaking ties. The curve accumulates ranked destination shares. Log bars use log(1 + CAD value) on a stated $0-to-largest-value scale; monetary labels remain actual values. Treemap areas and HS6 widths use untransformed values.

      Reconciliation and reproducibility

      The builder verifies exact integer-dollar equality for destination totals, Alberta HS4/HS6 totals, each country's HS4/HS6 totals, and every country-HS4's HS6 children. It independently compares every destination against the annual Alberta values in the separate HS2 source. Values are nonnegative. Approximate prior targets are checks only.

      Limits of interpretation

      • Current-dollar domestic merchandise exports are not services exports or quantities. Price changes can change dollar values.
      • Province of origin is not necessarily the exporter's headquarters or port of exit.
      • Destination does not necessarily identify final consumption location.
      • HS4 combines multiple HS6 products. National/special provisions are included in totals.
      • Small values remain subject to the source's disclosure and reporting characteristics. This extract is a snapshot and may differ from later revisions.
      • This page describes observed trade; it does not forecast market opportunity.
