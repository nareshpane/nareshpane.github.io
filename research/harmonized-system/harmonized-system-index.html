Research collection · Four connected studies

Harmonized System, Tariffs and Trade

A four-part collection moving from international commodity classification to Canadian tariff treatment, Alberta trade patterns, and applied trade-data analysis.

  • Harmonized System (HS) for Canada
    Start with commodity classification. Explore the sections, chapters, headings and six-digit subheadings in the CBSA Customs Tariff, then see how Canada adds national detail.
  • Section 338 HS4/HS6 Export Exposure
    Explore HS4 product categories and their HS6 subcategories, provincial Canada-U.S. domestic exports, and Section 338 exposure intensity.
  • Alberta’s Export Atlas
    Explore Alberta’s 2025 export destinations, HS4 product headings and HS6 subheadings through linked interactive views.
  • Practical HS Data Analysis in Python, R, and SQL Planned
    Turn classification into analysis with reproducible workflows for aggregating, joining and comparing trade data in Python, R and SQL.

Research drafts: Page 1 contains the classification explorer and Page 2 an annual export interactive. Page 3 explores Alberta’s observed exports; Page 4 outlines future work. This independent collection is not a CBSA or Government of Canada website.
