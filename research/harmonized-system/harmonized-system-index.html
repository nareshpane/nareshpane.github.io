Harmonized System (HS), Tariffs and Trade

📅Created: October 3-9, 2026
🧰Harness: Codex on Windows
Model: GPT-6.1 Sol
🧠Reasoning: High

A four-part collection moving from international commodity classification to Canadian tariff treatment, Alberta trade patterns, and applied trade-data analysis and prediction using several machine-learning techniques.

Each of the six pages covers a distinct topic and was developed through agentic implementation using Codex with GPT-6.1 Sol. Across these sessions, issues involving calculations, formatting, page layout, descriptions, redundant content, and visual aesthetics required repeated prompts and iterative corrections. The work involved approximately 45 hours of agentic sessions over seven days, from October 3 to October 9, 2026, inclusive.

  • Harmonized System (HS) for Canada
    Interactively explore Canada's HS hierarchy, from sections to HS6 subheadings, and examine Canadian tariff items, statistical suffixes and tariff details.
  • Canada and Provinces: HS6 Product Exposure and HS4 Section 338 Exposure Intensity
    Explore HS4 headings and HS6 products, Canadian provincial and territorial domestic exports to the United States, and Section 338 product exposure and HS4 exposure intensity.
  • Canada and Provincial Domestic Exports by HS4 and HS6 (2025)
    Explore Canada's and each province's 2025 domestic exports through ranked HS4 headings and stacked HS6 product breakdowns.
  • Alberta's Export Atlas
    Interactively explore Alberta's 2025 domestic exports across destination markets, HS4 headings and HS6 subheadings, including product composition and concentration across markets.
  • Machine Learning for Trade-Sector Prediction
    Compare six models predicting Alberta's 2025 exports by destination market and HS4 product, using trade, economic and geographic variables and examining observed results, prediction errors and assumptions.
  • Inside Six Machine Learning Models: Mathematics, Data, and Predictions
    An educational draft with the complete 336-row teaching dataset, step-by-step mathematical derivations, six fitted model demonstrations, observed 2024 prediction comparisons, and explicitly illustrative 2025 scenarios.
