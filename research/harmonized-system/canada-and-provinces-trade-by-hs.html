        Read the hierarchy

        Place first. Product detail next.

        Domestic exports

        Goods grown, extracted, produced or manufactured in Canada, including foreign goods materially transformed here, and exported abroad. Re-exports are excluded. Province or territory refers to the reported origin of production. Statistics Canada definitions.

        HS4 → HS6

        Four-digit headings group related merchandise; six-digit subheadings add product detail. Codes are text: chapter 06 → heading 0601 → subheading 060110. Chapters 98 and 99 contain Canadian special provisions.

        Ranking & composition

        Headings are ordered by observed 2025 domestic export value. The HS6 segments add exactly to their parent HS4 total. They describe export composition; these values are not forecasts, tariff exposure estimates or measures of commercial opportunity.

        Methodology, data reconciliation & description sources

        Twelve months, all reported destinations

        The local CIMT domestic-export archive contains monthly, customs-based records. The builder selects January–December 2025 from the HS6 file ODPFN018_202512N.csv and sums destination countries and U.S. states once. There are no annual summary rows or world-destination totals. Unknown or unspecified destinations, high seas and any separately reported destination categories remain included. Quantities and unlike measurement units are not combined.

        Canada and thirteen exhaustive origins

        The active StatCan province lookup identifies all ten provinces and three territories; the source has no Canada-origin or residual-origin category. Canada is derived from those thirteen mutually exclusive identifiers. Every HS6, HS4 and grand total reconciles exactly. The independent HS8 source is checked against HS6 for each origin and product, and the HS2 source against HS6 for each month, origin and chapter. These checks establish consistency within this archive; no independent national observation is available.

        HS4 total = sum of all positive HS6 child values

        Only positive annual values enter the rankings. Zero-value records remain in the audit. Ties use ascending product code with consecutive ordinal ranks. Codes retain leading zeros. The builder stops on unexpected duplicates, repeated observation keys, missing or suppressed values, incompatible codes or reconciliation differences.

        Destination countries

        The destination ranking sums the same 2025 HS6 records by origin, HS4 and official destination code, including U.S. states once. Country and territory names use the active CIMT country lookup. All-country aggregates are absent. Unknown or unspecified destinations, high seas, the legacy Pacific Islands category and Canada are excluded from the individual international ranking; all four have zero value in this snapshot. Each country share uses the full origin-HS4 total. Every individual-destination sum reconciles exactly; omitted Top N destinations appear as an unranked total. Values are never rescaled.

        Official labels, traced by code

        HS6 descriptions come from ODPF_4_HS6XDesc.TXT, using validity intervals overlapping 2025. HS2 and geography names use the associated lookups. HS4 descriptions prefer Statistics Canada's 2025 Canadian Export Classification, with unsplit .00 rows used only for headings with a single active child. Where exact-year website retrieval was unavailable, the retained official CBSA T2026-2 heading wording supplements the labels. It supplies descriptions only; every export observation remains from 2025. The detail panel names the source of each heading, and the audit lists all later-label supplements.
