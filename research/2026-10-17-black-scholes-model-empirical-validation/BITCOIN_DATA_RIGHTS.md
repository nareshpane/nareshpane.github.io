# Bitcoin academic data: current rights and source record

Reviewed October 17, 2026, after inspection of the three manually supplied CSVs. This supersedes the earlier access-pending state. No source dataset was re-downloaded.

The [official UWA record](https://research-repository.uwa.edu.au/en/datasets/deribits-options-data/) labels each attachment CC BY, names Hoang and Baur, and dates the deposit to April 2020. The exact CC version is not stated and is not invented. The [associated paper](https://doi.org/10.1002/fut.22144) identifies Deribit trade API and Bitcoincharts provenance. Citation: Hoang, L., & Baur, D. (2020), *Deribit’s Options Data*, The University of Western Australia, [doi:10.26182/5eabbcb4fbf28](https://doi.org/10.26182/5eabbcb4fbf28).

The actual files are plain CSVs containing market and researcher-derived fields. No embedded additional restrictive notice or account/person identifier was found. Reuse here relies on the repository grant for material it validly covers: scholarly calculations, attributed aggregate results and twenty limited explanatory transactions. Mathematical/statistical transformations are independently identified; no author, university or exchange endorsement is implied. Full raw and processed datasets remain ignored and locally stored.

The [January 2021 exchange announcement](https://insights.deribit.com/exchange-updates/deribit-updates-terms-of-service-as-of-29-january-2021/) dates the addition of a market-data clause after the deposit. Current [exchange membership terms](https://support.deribit.com/hc/en-us/articles/25944532191645-Deribit-Exchange-Membership-Terms-Deribit-FZE) address platform/member use. No concrete evidence reviewed establishes that those later conditions retroactively invalidate this historical university grant. No direct exchange data collection is performed. The remaining specificity limits are the unspecified CC version and unprovided upstream authorization documentation; these are disclosed, not represented as an automatic prohibition or as additional written approval.

The local scoped review record separately documents processing and publication bases. No permission email was sent, and no grant is fabricated by that record. If a conflicting notice or nonpublic field is later identified, its affected use must be reassessed. Attribution is not a substitute for rights the licensor did not hold.
