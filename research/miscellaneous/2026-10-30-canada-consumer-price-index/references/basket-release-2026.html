Consumer Price Index: New basket weights, 2025

Text - Selected Tables Related information Previous release PDF (109 KB)

Released: 2026-06-15

Updated basket weights for the goods and services in the Consumer Price Index (CPI), representing consumer spending patterns in 2025, are now available. The 2025 basket will combine expenditure data from the Household Final Consumption Expenditures, the Survey of Household Spending and alternative data sources to account for shifts in spending.

The updated weights will be incorporated into the May 2026 CPI, which will be released on June 22, 2026. They will replace the previous weights, which were based on 2024 expenditure patterns.

The weight reference period for the new basket is 2025. The index base period for the all-items CPI remains 2002=100.

For more detailed information, consult the document entitled "An Analysis of the 2026 Consumer Price Index Basket Update, Based on 2025 Expenditures."
