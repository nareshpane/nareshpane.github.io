## Visual and numerical corrections

Allocation uses seeded shuffle with exactly 270/270. Balance uses fixed synthetic age, migraine days and prior use. Counterfactual table has 12 rows and a conceptual reveal; three response-type cards use favorable-outcome coding. Keep both. Sampling lab uses 60 normal-approximate estimates with fixed SE, not actual binomial repeated trials; clarify and use exact normal draws. Fisher lab adds 400 permutations per click; change raw extreme share to plus-one p-value to avoid misleading zero estimates. Power needs baseline risk. Static CI geometry and unlabeled subgroup plot need correction. Cluster figure displays 48 icons while caption says 80; correct caption. Fixed six-slot block illustrations do not establish variable block sizes; relabel. Existing animations are event-triggered (no autoplay); add a finite allocation replay with reduced-motion support. No timeline sliders exist or are required.
