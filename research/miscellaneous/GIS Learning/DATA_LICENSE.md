This project contains materials with different origins and rights. This summary is not a replacement for the current official terms.

## Original TransLink data

The raw archive and extracted GTFS tables are provided by TransLink:

- Official feed: <https://gtfs-static.translink.ca/gtfs/google_transit.zip>
- GTFS-specific terms: <https://www.translink.ca/about-us/doing-business-with-translink/app-developer-resources/gtfs/gtfs-data>
- Terms accessed: 2026-08-15

The GTFS-specific terms state that TransLink grants a "limited, revocable and non-exclusive license" to use, reproduce, and redistribute the data in accordance with those terms. They also state that TransLink retains its right, title, interest, and intellectual-property rights in the data. The terms include attribution, disclaimer, use, redistribution, and possible commercial-use conditions.

No standard public-domain, Creative Commons, Open Government Licence, ODbL, or SPDX licence for this feed was identified. The TransLink data must not be described as public domain, copyright-free, or covered by an MIT licence. Consult the live official terms before use or redistribution, especially for commercial use.

## Processed and derived data

The GeoJSON and CSV files in `data/processed/` are derived from the TransLink GTFS data. Processing changes their representation but does not erase TransLink's rights or the applicable source terms. Redistribute and present these files with the attribution in [ATTRIBUTION.md](ATTRIBUTION.md) and in accordance with TransLink's current terms.
