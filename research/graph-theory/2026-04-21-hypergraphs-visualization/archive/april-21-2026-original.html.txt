      <h1>Hypergraph Demo</h1>
      <p class="lead">
        A hypergraph is a generalization of an ordinary graph. In a graph, every edge joins exactly two vertices.
        In a hypergraph, a single hyperedge may join two vertices, three vertices, four vertices, or more.
      </p>

      <div class="panel note">
        <strong>Analytical definition.</strong><br/>
        A hypergraph is a pair $H=(X,E)$ where $X$ is a finite vertex set and $E$ is a family of subsets of $X$.
        Each element of $E$ is called a <em>hyperedge</em>.
        Thus ordinary graphs are the special case in which every hyperedge has size $2$.
      </div>

      <div class="panel warn">
        <strong>Algebraic notation.</strong><br/>
        If $X=\{v_1,v_2,v_3,v_4\}$ and $E=\{e_1,e_2\}$ with
        $e_1=\{v_1,v_2,v_3\}$ and $e_2=\{v_3,v_4\}$, then the same hypergraph may also be written as
        $$H=(X,E),\qquad X=\{v_1,v_2,v_3,v_4\},\qquad E=\{\{v_1,v_2,v_3\},\{v_3,v_4\}\}.$$
      </div>

      <div class="mini-grid">
        <div class="mini-box">
          <div class="k">How to read each PNG</div>
          <div class="v">Each picture shows one hypergraph in four views, ending with a simple-graph translation.</div>
        </div>
        <div class="mini-box">
          <div class="k">Simple-graph translation</div>
          <div class="v">The last panel uses the 2-section: vertices are adjacent when they lie in a common hyperedge.</div>
        </div>
      </div>

      <h2>How the examples are organized</h2>
      <p>
        The ten PNGs below are ordered from simpler to more structurally involved examples.
        Early examples are graph-like, while later ones show larger hyperedges, overlaps, singleton edges, and isolated vertices.
      </p>
      <ul class="tight-list">
        <li>The folder placeholder used throughout is <code>hypergraph_outputs/</code>.</li>
        <li>Each example lists the hypergraph algebraically before showing the PNG path.</li>
        <li>The captions are intentionally short so the page stays clean and easy to scan.</li>
      </ul>
